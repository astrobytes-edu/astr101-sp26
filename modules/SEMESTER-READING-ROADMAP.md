## Semester Narrative Arc

The course tells one story in three acts:

| Module | Theme | Central Question | Key Skill |
|--------|-------|------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Sky & Foundations | "How do we locate and decode light?" | Geometry + light physics |
| 2 | Stars | "How do stars work and evolve?" | Applying physics to stellar interiors |
| 3 | Cosmos | "How did the universe become what it is?" | Scaling up from stars to galaxies to the universe |

**Through-line:** Pretty pictures → measurements → models → inferences

### Key Concepts to Emphasize
- **Hydrostatic equilibrium** — gravity vs pressure (the competition that drives everything)
- **Nuclear fusion** — why it requires extreme conditions
- **Mass determines fate** — the most important single idea in stellar evolution
- **We are stardust** — elements heavier than helium come from stars

### Your Research Integration Opportunities

| 16 | L30 | Dark Energy & the Fate of the Universe | Accelerating expansion, open/flat/closed |

**Final Exam:** Friday May 8, 10:30 AM

### Key Concepts to Emphasize
- **Galaxies as stellar populations** — connecting Module 2 (stars) to Module 3 (collections of stars)
- **Dark matter** — we infer it from orbital motion (just like we infer stellar mass!)
- **Hubble's Law** — redshift ↔ distance ↔ expansion
- **The CMB** — a direct photograph of the early universe
- **Unanswered questions** — dark matter identity, dark energy, Hubble tension
