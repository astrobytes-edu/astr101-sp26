# Homework 6 Design

**Date:** 2026-04-08

**Goal:** Build a 10-problem homework covering Lectures 18-21 that feels like a coherent review of stellar birth, evolution, death, and compact remnants.

## Recommended structure

Use a **Lifecycle Arc**:

1. star formation
2. pre-main-sequence and main-sequence evolution
3. stellar death and supernova reasoning
4. neutron stars and black holes

This keeps the homework from feeling like four disconnected mini-assignments.

## Selected required problems

1. **Lecture 18:** `P2` - The Jeans Criterion
2. **Lecture 18:** `P5` - Observing a Hidden Protostar
3. **Lecture 19:** `P1` - Main-Sequence Lifetime Calculation
4. **Lecture 19:** `P2` - The Red-Giant Sun
5. **Lecture 19:** `P4` - Cluster Age from Main-Sequence Turnoff
6. **Lecture 20:** `P3` - Chandrasekhar Limit
7. **Lecture 20:** `P8` - Supernova Energy Budget
8. **Lecture 20:** `P9` - Type Ia vs. Type II: Observable Differences
9. **Lecture 21:** `P2` - Schwarzschild Radius
10. **Lecture 21:** `C4` - Multi-Messenger Astronomy

## Why this set works

- It includes all four lectures.
- It balances conceptual, calculation, and synthesis work.
- It gives students repeated practice with Observable -> Model -> Inference reasoning.
- It includes one modern astronomy capstone (`Lecture 21: C4`) and one comparative supernova synthesis prompt (`Lecture 20: P9`).
- It remains demanding but manageable because most problems are core problems, not challenge problems.

## Difficulty profile

- Early problems: entry and review
- Middle problems: multi-step astrophysical interpretation
- Final two problems: synthesis and transfer
